What's the goal of the project?

Auth System's goal is to make a complex ecosystem of different apps and different authors, helping to bring things togheter without any effort, but still keeping all of your private data safe.

How could it be used?

Case 1 - Social media apps:
Have some posts you made in App 1 and want to migrate them to App 2? Link your accounts, give App 2 the permissions to access App 1's posts, then import them effortlessly!
Case 2 - Chat apps:
Don't know your best friend's username in App 2, but already friended him in App 1? Link the accounts, give the permissions and sync your friends!
Case 3 - Mixed apps:
Want to import your files from a cloud service to your messaging app? Link the accounts, give the access to your files and send them easily!

How can I delete my account?

You can download all of your data from all apps (restricted folders will be added soon, to guarantee the app's safety too), or delete specific data from the dashboard. If you want to delete your Auth System account, then you can find the button on the dashboard.

How does this work?

Imagine a tree. You've got the trunk, all of the different branches, and then you've got the leaves. Try to immagine your account as the trunk, your apps as the brunches and your data as the leaves! Just like trees, your apps can communicate with each other to create a seamless ecosystem!


How is my data treated?

Encryption

Your data is encrypted, forcing every app to get data from the filtered proxy system!

All of your data is separated in different folders, one for each account and app. When an app wants to access another one's data, it won't have direct access. The data will be proxied instead, so it will be read by Auth System, then sent individually following the permissions allowed by the user.
About encryption: All of your data (user info, app data and everything on your account, except for logs) is safely encrypted and the keys are separated from the users folder. Since all the data gets decrypted from the proxy, it is required to pass from the proxy's filters before downloading the data. Data is encrypted with 2 different keys, a specific randomly generated one for your account, and a common one.

What does connecting apps do?

Once you connect an app, it becomes like an extension of your account. You can access its data from other apps, making them able to interact without any problems. All of the data transfers are processed with filters, so your data can always stay safe.
Remember that you have to give the permissions for the apps to write or read each other's data, and the app has to allow the action. An app could have a friend list, still not allowing sharing it with other apps.
A shared folder is also present in the system, no apps need any permissions to read or write to this folder.
All of the actions made through the API are logged in a specific, unencrypted logs folder. There's one log for each day, and they are registered to allow you to protect your account from unsafe apps!

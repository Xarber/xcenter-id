Change Username

Careful!

When changing your username, remember the following:

  • You will be signed out from all sessions.
    (This includes both devices and apps)
  • You will always need to use the new username when signing in.
  • You will need to update your saved passwords to the new username.
  • You will NOT need to change your password.
  • You will NOT need to change your 2FA info.
  • You will NOT need to change your encryption keys.
